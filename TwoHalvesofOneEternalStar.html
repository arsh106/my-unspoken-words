Unspoken: Poems of Love and Memory

Two Halves of One Eternal Star

About this poem

Two Halves of One Eternal Star is a deeply emotional poem about two rare souls bound by a connection that transcends distance, time, and ordinary understanding.

It explores the mystery of mirrored souls, the tension between the masculine and feminine, and the inner conflict that arises when one soul reflects truths the other is not yet ready to face. Beneath the pain of separation lies a deeper spiritual journey, one shaped by awakening, surrender, and the search for light.

The poem speaks of a love that does not disappear with distance. When one soul hurts, the other feels it. When one finds happiness, the other smiles. Their unseen connection becomes a sacred current flowing between them, even across the miles.

Ultimately, this is a poem about surrendering to a bond that feels written beyond this lifetime, and about two halves of one eternal star, separated by the vastness of distance, yet forever carrying the same light within.

We are rare souls upon this earth.

The Divine split us apart
and gave us two different vessels.

We are mirrors of each other,
which makes our connection
almost too profound to bear.

We are here on a spiritual mission,
not merely to endure
a painful heartbreak and separation.

The masculine part of me
runs from my feminine part.

He feels that I reflect
the inner self he longs to avoid.

I am too raw for him to hold.

I see through the language of emotion
and accept what simply is.

He sees through the lens
of a controlled, analytical mind.
He does not simply accept.
He questions.

He is relentlessly at war
with his own mind,
blind to who I am to him.

My heart is wounded,
bleeding endlessly.

When will he unveil his true self?

I wish he would reach the light
before I breathe my last,
and set me free from this unbearable pain
that lingers within my heart
like molten lava,
burning through the deepest chambers
of my soul.

If he is hurt, I bleed.
If he is happy, I smile.

Although we are distant,
miles apart,
our souls remain bound
beyond the limits of space and time.

A wave of energy flows between us,
sacred and endless current.

There is no escape
from this divine design.

So I surrender
to the bond that holds us.

I surrender
to the truth of what we are.

We are twin flames,
eternally connected,
two halves of one eternal star,
separated by the vastness of distance,
yet forever carrying
the same light within.

Arshia Sultana
