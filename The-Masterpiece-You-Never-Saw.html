The Masterpiece You Never Saw

About this poem

The Masterpiece You Never Saw is a reflection on the person we create in our hearts when we fall for someone—the version of them shaped by our hopes, dreams, and deepest desires. Like an artist painting on a blank canvas, the speaker gradually realizes that the portrait she created was not only about the person she loved, but also about discovering the depth of her own heart.

Even without knowing what the other person truly felt, the connection left something meaningful behind: a transformed self. This poem is a quiet expression of gratitude for an imagined love that became an unexpected journey of self-discovery

When I met you,
I began to idealize the version of you
that you revealed to me.

I came to this connection
with my paints and brush,
ready to create a masterpiece
you never knew existed.

I made you my canvas,
painting the portrait of the man
I desired most,
someone brave, honest, true,
and devoted to love.

I sketched the blueprint
upon your walls,
while you became my training ground.

Through you,
I learned how to love
with the fullest capacity of my heart,
to idealize,
to hope,
to dream.

I do not know
what emotions you carried for me,
or what I meant to you.

Yet I need you to know this:

Your connection transformed me
into someone I never knew
was waiting within me.

And for that,
I thank you
from the bottom of my heart,
for helping me discover
the depth of who I could become.

Arshia Sultana
