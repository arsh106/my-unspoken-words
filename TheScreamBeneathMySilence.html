Unspoken: Poems of Love and Memory

The Scream Beneath My Silence

About this poem

The Scream Beneath My Silence explores the quiet devastation of carrying profound emotions that have nowhere to go. Through the imagery of a restless ocean, a locked door, and a scream trapped beneath a mask of normalcy, the poem portrays the invisible weight of unspoken love, longing, and sorrow.

It speaks of the loneliness of feeling deeply while struggling to find understanding in the world around us. Beneath an ordinary exterior lies an emotional tempest, where memories linger, tenderness remains, and words fail to contain the magnitude of the heart.

At its core, this poem is about the exhaustion of holding back an ocean of emotions and the human longing to have one's pain witnessed, understood, and treated with compassion.

Some oceans cannot be seen. Some silences carry entire lifetimes.

Dear Lion, the King of My Heart

Do you know
how the resonance of your crisis
reaches my tender heart?

Perhaps when you are lost
in the depths of turbulence,
I feel your pain
without knowing why.

My heart sinks into turmoil,
my body restless,
gripped by an inexplicable fear,
anxiety without a name or reason.

Thoughts of you
bring tears that refuse to cease.
In solitude, I weep,
yet wear a mask of normalcy,
swallowing my sorrow
day after day.

A scream claws at my throat,
desperate to break free.
It burns my chest,
tightens my jaw,
steals my breath
behind the calm exterior
I show the world.

My eyes hunger
for one glimpse of your face,
one moment
to behold the man
my heart still remembers.

When I open
the smallest window of my heart,
hoping for a sliver of understanding
from the human world,

they reduce my profound and sacred bond
to a cold, cynical explanation
and slam the door
on my vulnerable truth.

Some offer me pills
to silence my agony,
as though sorrow were an illness
and not a sacred wound
that longs to be understood and valued.

It feels like a violation
of everything I hold sacred.

I am carrying an ocean
behind a locked door,
day after day,
while no one in my physical world
seems willing to witness
the waves rising within me.

I keep trying to mend
what lies beyond my reach,
to force what cannot be forced,
to find a way
through what remains unresolved
and forever beyond my grasp.

And this love,
with nowhere to go,
no place to rest,
no arms to receive its tenderness,
has drained the last drops
of my weary soul.

Still, behind the silence,
beneath the mask,
beyond the tears
I hide from the world,

my heart carries
what words cannot contain.

An ocean of sorrow,
a lifetime of tenderness,
and a love
with nowhere to lay its head.

Arshia Sultana
