Unspoken: Poems of Love and Memory

Where His Memories Bloom

About this poem

Where His Memories Bloom is a reflection on how words can preserve a love and memories that time could never completely erase.

The garden in this poem is not a physical place. It is the quiet space within the heart where unspoken words, cherished memories, and enduring emotions continue to live. Each flower represents something once left unsaid, while the passing of decades has transformed a single chapter of life into an entire book of memories.

🎵 "Harp" - atlasaudio (Pixabay Music)

I plant flowers in my garden,
not from seeds,
but from words.

These are the words
that never reached my lips.

Decades have passed,
and a single chapter of my life
has grown into a book.

Within its pages,
he breathes,
and his memories come alive.

His fiery sun nourishes the garden,
while my gentle wind
carries its fragrance afar.

This garden is our sanctuary,
our little home of memories,
where what was never spoken
continues to bloom.

Arshia Sultana
