Unspoken: Poems of Love and Memory

Metamorphosis

About this poem

Metamorphosis is a poem about courage, transformation, and the freedom that comes from releasing long-held emotions into the world. After years of silence, hidden feelings, and an inner struggle, the Butterfly finally lays down her armor, removes her mask, and allows her truth to take flight.

Trusting the Divine with what lies beyond her control, she begins to reclaim her peace and energy. Though she has emerged from her cocoon with newfound courage, a flickering light of hope remains in her heart. That hope belongs to her Lion, the king of her heart, whom she wishes to find his way home.

This poem captures the delicate balance between letting go and holding on to hope, surrendering to the Divine while allowing love to remain a part of one's story. Sometimes, metamorphosis does not mean forgetting the past. It means finding the courage to spread your wings and embrace the freedom to become who you were always meant to be.

I have reached the culmination point,
where I can no longer play hide-and-seek
or keep secrets in the darkest corners.
I knew I had to break
the long silence.

So I took off my armor
and left the mask behind.

With courage and bravery,
I released my words into the world,
trusting them to reach
where they are meant to be.

I revealed the very trail
that leads to my nest.
What happens next
is no longer in my hands.

I leave everything
in the hands of the Divine,
who knows the pain
I have endured for years.

Today, I am free,
and I will reclaim my energy.
I have truly undergone a metamorphosis,
from a quiet cocoon
to a fully fledged,
fluttering butterfly.

Yet, there remains
a flickering light of hope
within my heart.

May my Lion,
the king of my heart,
find his way home.

Arshia Sultana
