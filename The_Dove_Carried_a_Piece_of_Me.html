Unspoken: Poems of Love and Memory

The Dove Carried a Piece of Me

About this poem

The Dove Carried a Piece of Me is a meditation on an eternal bond that seems to exist beyond distance, time, and even the boundaries of this world.

The dove becomes a messenger between two souls, carrying a small piece of one heart toward the other. The moon watches from afar, witnessing a love that remains unspoken, hidden beneath years of silence and separation.

This poem speaks of a final homecoming, not necessarily in this lifetime, and not necessarily in the way the heart once dreamed. It explores the belief that some connections cannot truly be broken, even when life, fear, and worldly circumstances keep two souls apart.

At its heart, this is a poem about love that survives silence, separation, and time, a love that continues to burn quietly, waiting for the moment when all secrets disappear and two souls can finally recognize their eternal bond .

The day the dove spread its wings
to carry a piece of my heart to you,

the universe confirmed:

You will return home,
to your twin flame,
your other half.

Perhaps not with joy,
perhaps not with bliss,

but you will come
to hold her one last time
when she is all set
to fade away from your history,
leaving the love in her heart
burning forever.

She is sacrificing
every bit of herself
to keep that love alive for you.

The moon is the witness.
It watches over us
from a distance.

Perhaps that day
will be a fateful night
beneath the moonlight.

You will hold
her still body,
and her truth
will finally come alive.

There will be no more secrets,
no more hiding,
no more hide-and-seek.

Do not shed your tears, my love.
You are a lion,
majestic and proud,
afraid to reveal your vulnerability.

Your butterfly
will come fluttering
when your time comes.

She made a pact
with the Divine
decades ago.

And when such a bond
is written beyond this world,
no worldly fear
can separate you from her.

You will be free
to reunite with her
in the other realm—
the place
you call your eternal home.

Arshia Sultana
