Unspoken: Poems of Love and Memory

The Last Word

About this poem

This poem is about closure, release, and letting go of a chapter that once meant so much.

Some stories do not end with the words we hoped to hear. Sometimes, silence becomes the ending. After carrying the remnants of an old love for many years, I finally reached the place where I could accept what could not be changed.

The olive branch represents my final gesture of peace. What followed was silence, and I chose to accept that silence rather than continue reaching for an answer.

This poem is my farewell to what once was. Not because it never mattered, but because it mattered enough to deserve a peaceful ending.

I release the hurt, the unanswered questions, and everything that no longer belongs in my present.

I close the chapter with a quiet heart and open hands, grateful for what once was and free to walk forward.

Some chapters are not meant to be rewritten. They are meant to be released.

Without prior notice,
from the silence, a soul appeared.

He made me feel seen,
gave me countless reasons to laugh.

Our hearts sang in a single chord,
but our values were never aligned,
slowly drifting into separate skies,
like the distant sun and a fading moon.

I reached toward a distant vision;
he followed a path that wounded us both.
Duty and life's burdens kept my heart restrained,
while resentment carried him farther from himself.

Unable to make peace with his choices,
he placed their weight upon my shoulders.
After a long silence,
I offered friendship instead of judgment.

I gave him an olive branch
to begin anew,
yet he answered with harsh silence
and declined my request to be friends.

Perhaps it was sweet revenge,
a wound he wished to inflict upon me.

I imagine this last wound he gave me
was his victory.

Yet, deep down,
I know there was something he held for me.
Whatever feeling he conceals,
today, I release the remnants of our story.

The scales are finally balanced.
He remains in bitterness;
I choose to live in growth.

I accept his silence
as the last word.

This is my final poem,
my final tribute
to our chaotic love story.

Today, I make a vow:

I will no longer hold my pen
in his remembrance.

With a quiet heart and open hands,
I close this chapter.
I wish not to meet him again,
not in this lifetime
nor across any lifetime.

Now I am free to walk forward,
no longer looking back.
And I will remember
that he was once
a part of my story.

Nothing more.
Nothing less.

And I am finally free.

Arshia Sultana
