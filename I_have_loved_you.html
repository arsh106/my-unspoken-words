Unspoken: Poems of Love and Memory

I Have Loved You...

About this poem

I Have Loved You... is a reflection on a love that endured through distance, absence, and silence. It speaks of loving someone without asking to be loved in return, remembering a connection that once felt close, and carrying that love through two decades of separation.

Sometimes love does not end simply because two people lose their way to each other. Sometimes it remains quietly, even when there is no bridge left to cross and no certainty that two hearts will ever find their way back.

This poem is about that kind of love: a love that remains, even when reconnection may never be possible.

I have loved you

without ever needing
you to love me back.

I have loved you

even when you became reluctant
to bridge the distance between us.

I have loved you

when you were with me, yet lost,
present in body
but distant in soul.

I have loved you

even when I had to fly away,
as graduation called me
away from the campus,
and you resented my leaving.

I have loved you

even when you left me
without a word.

I have loved you

through two decades
of your absence.

And I have loved you

when silence
was all I received from you.

Through leaving,
across distance,
through your absence,
and in silence,
I have loved you.

And I still love you,

even knowing
that we may never
find our way back to each other.

Arshia Sultana
