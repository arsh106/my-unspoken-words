Unspoken: Poems of Love and Memory

The Depths They Cannot See

About this poem

The Depths They Cannot See is a reflection on enduring love, personal conviction, and the courage to live beyond society’s expectations. It speaks for those whose choice to remain single is misunderstood and whose hearts hold a depth of devotion that cannot be measured by conventional standards.

Through the imagery of empty rooms, deep waters, fallen leaves, and awakened light, the poem explores the difference between how others perceive a life and the sacred truth held within it. It is a declaration of self-respect, spiritual growth, and faith in God’s divine design.

Some journeys cannot be explained to the world. Some truths can only be understood by the soul that carries them.

I have carried you in my heart for so long,
Tending this love in the quiet of my solitude
And vowed to remain yours, forever.

The world has mocked my singlehood,
Mistaking my choice for an empty life,
As though love were interchangeable,
And loneliness could be cured
By filling a space with just anyone.

They urge me to find a man,
To settle down and follow society’s timeline,
As if my heart were an empty room
Waiting for a stranger to move in.

But they do not understand
That my spirit remains deeply anchored
In a love I have carried through the years,
A devotion that cannot be replaced
Simply to satisfy the world’s expectations.

They see no strength in my solitude,
No depth in the loyalty I have nurtured,
No spiritual growth in the truth I hold.
What they cannot understand, they judge,
Measuring my life by standards
That were never meant to define me.

I am weary of their unsolicited advice,
Their careless words and shallow judgments,
For they speak of love as though it were
A choice made merely to escape being alone.

I will honor the love I carry
And trust in God’s divine design.
Their opinions fall like dry leaves,
Scattered by winds I no longer fear.

They do not know the depths of the waters
Through which my soul has journeyed,
Nor have they seen the light
That awakened something sacred within me.

Arshia Sultana
