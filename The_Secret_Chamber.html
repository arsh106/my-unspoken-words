Unspoken: Poems of Love and Memory

The Secret Chamber

About this poem

The Secret Chamber is a poem about love that survives silence, memory that refuses to disappear, and the hidden truths we carry within us.

It explores the quiet space between two people who once knew each other, yet left so much unsaid. Beneath the mystery of another person, we sometimes sense a pain they cannot reveal, even when they never allow us close enough to understand it.

The butterfly in this poem represents the delicate self I once allowed the world to see. Beneath its wings lives another woman: the Empress, who has learned that love can be offered without demands, expectations, or conditions.

The secret chamber is the heart itself, a place where unspoken truths can rest safely, untouched by judgment, and held with tenderness.

This poem is an invitation to come without fear, without masks, and without hiding.

It has been decades since I last saw you,
yet I have held you in my heart
since the moment I first met you.

In my quiet hours,
I remember the gift of your memories
with love and warmth.

I whisper your name in solitude,
yet the feeling of your absence
still stings me deeply.

Perhaps it is because
I have always known you
as a boy who hides
beneath the shrouds of mystery.

You did not trust me,
Yet somehow,
I felt the pain you were hiding in secret.

You never noticed
how my eyes saw the sorrow
lingering within yours.

I pretended to be
nothing more than a fluttering butterfly,
so you would not feel threatened.

You failed to see
the real “me”
beneath the flapping wings
the woman who gazed through her mask,
carrying questions
about the pain you kept hidden.

Boy,
I carry the energy of the Empress.
I demand nothing from you.
I have learned
how to give without conditions,
without expectations,
without asking anything in return.

I could have been
your safe haven,
the place where your truth
never needed to hide.

Your container
of unspoken truths
where everything
you could never say
could rest safely
within the secret chamber
of my heart
and remain there
forever.

And now,
I invite you
to return to my realm once again.

Come without fear.
Come without masks.
Come with all the truths
you once kept hidden.

I promise,
you will not be disappointed.

Arshia Sultana
